Who We Are

More Than a Gym. A Movement.

Founded in 2015 by National Champion bodybuilder Vikram Singh, IronForge was born from a single conviction: that every person deserves access to elite-level coaching and facilities — not just professional athletes.

What started as a 2,000 sq ft training floor has grown into Pune's most respected premium fitness destination — 5,000 sq ft of world-class equipment, 15 certified coaches, 2,500 active members, and over a thousand body transformation success stories.

PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

IronForge Fitness Club ("we", "us", "our") collects information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, payment information, and fitness goals when you register for membership, book sessions, or make purchases.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process memberships, bookings, and orders
  • To communicate with you about services, offers, and updates
  • To personalize your experience and training programs
  • To send promotional materials (with your consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Sheets and our systems. We use industry-standard encryption and access controls. Payment screenshots are stored only until order confirmation and deleted within 30 days.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (e.g., delivery partners for orders) or as required by law.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@ironforge.in to exercise these rights.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@ironforge.in | +91 98765 43210

TERMS &
CONDITIONS

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Membership Terms

By joining IronForge, you agree to abide by all gym rules and regulations. Membership is non-transferable. Memberships can be frozen for medical reasons with a doctor's certificate, subject to a ₹200 administrative fee.

2. Payment & Refunds

All membership fees are payable in advance. Refunds are not provided for unused membership periods. Product returns are accepted within 7 days if unopened and undamaged.

3. Health & Safety

Members must disclose any medical conditions that may affect their ability to exercise safely. IronForge is not liable for injuries resulting from improper use of equipment or failure to follow trainer instructions.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Wear appropriate workout attire at all times
  • Wipe down equipment after use
  • Re-rack weights after use
  • Respect all staff and fellow members
  • No aggressive behavior or language

5. Amendments

IronForge reserves the right to modify these terms with 30 days' notice. Continued membership after notice constitutes acceptance of changes.
